Connecting to Wi-Fi

  1. Open Settings → System → Wi-Fi. Left and right turn Wi-Fi on and off.
  2. Press A to see the networks and choose yours.
  3. Type the password on the on-screen keyboard: X switches between uppercase, lowercase and symbols.

Networks are remembered. With Wi-Fi connected the time sets itself.

Updating the system

  1. Connect the console to Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Settings → Tools → Update system.
  3. Confirm with A. The console restarts when it's done.

ROMs, saves and settings are left untouched. Every update is signed and checked before it is installed, and if the new system fails to start, the console goes back to the previous one by itself.
