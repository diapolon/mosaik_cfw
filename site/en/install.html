Mosaik lives entirely on the microSD. The console's internal storage is left alone: remove the card and the original system starts again.

1. Write the image

  1. Download the image for your model. There's no need to decompress it.
  2. Open balenaEtcher, choose the file and the microSD, then start writing. Etcher checks the copy by itself.

Everything on the card is erased. If your computer asks to format part of the card afterwards, say no.

2. First boot

  1. Turn the console off, insert the card and turn it on.
  2. The first time, the partition grows by itself to fill the card, up to 256 GB, so startup takes a little longer.
  3. The launcher appears. From then on, about 5 seconds is enough.

3. Set the date and language

  • Open the settings with Start.
  • Language is under Appearance; date, time and time zone are under System.
  • With Wi-Fi connected the time sets itself: see Wi-Fi and updates.

4. Copy your games

Turn the console off and connect the card to your computer: a drive called ARCADE appears. The Loading games guide explains where the files go.

Going back to the original system

Turn the console off and remove the card: the TrimUI system starts at the next power on, as before.
