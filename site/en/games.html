With the console off, connect the microSD to your computer. Everything is in the Arcade folder of the ARCADE drive.

ROMs and BIOS files are not included. Only use copies of games you have the right to use.

The folders

FolderWhat goes in it
Roms/FBNEOROMs for FinalBurn Neo, subfolders allowed (neogeo/, cps2/…)
Roms/MAME2003PROMs for MAME 2003-Plus
Biosneogeo.zip and other BIOS files
LinuxLinux games, one per folder
MediaGame images
MusicBackground music (MP3, OGG, FLAC, MOD)
SavesSaves

The Arcade folder is the same on both models: ROMs and saves can move from one card to the other.

Checking your ROMs

Game won't start? Settings → Tools → Verify ROMs checks that every zip contains the right files for its emulator. The game's details page (X) then shows the result, including any missing BIOS.

Covers and information

With Wi-Fi connected, Settings → Tools → Download images fetches screenshots, titles, marquees, genre and game history from ArcadeDB and the libretro thumbnails.

You can also copy images from your computer: Media/snap/, Media/title/ or Media/marquee/, as PNG, named after the ROM (romname.png).

If you delete games, Clean up images removes the images no longer needed.

Finding and sorting games

  • In the library, Select opens search, genre, collection and sorting.
  • Y adds a game to your favorites.
  • On the game's details page, X opens collections and genre change.
  • Settings → Games → Game genres renames or merges genres.
  • Duplicate games decides what to show when a game is in both FinalBurn Neo and MAME.
