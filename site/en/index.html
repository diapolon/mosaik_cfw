All your games, in your pocket.

A gaming system for TrimUI Smart Pro and Brick Pro: arcade and Linux games. It runs from the microSD and leaves the console's internal storage alone: take the card out and everything is back as it was.

What's inside

  • FinalBurn Neo and MAME 2003-Plus: Neo Geo, CPS1, CPS2 and most of the arcade classics.
  • Linux games, with an in-game menu and sleep.
  • Boots in about 5 seconds, sleep with the power button.
  • Built-in Wi-Fi for covers, game history, time and updates.
  • Themes, languages, LEDs and video filters you can configure.
  • Signed updates, with automatic return to the previous version.
