Settings open with Start and are organized in five categories.

Display and sound

Brightness, VolumeAlso apply in games.
MusicBackground music from the files in the Music folder. A skips to the next track.
LEDsLight effect: off, steady, breathing or pulse. On low battery they blink red.
LED colour and brightness“Theme” uses the main colour of the chosen theme.

Appearance

Theme, VariantTessera, Cabinato and Onda, with their color schemes.
LanguageItalian, English, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese.
Thumbnails per rowHow many covers each library row shows.
Home coversSize of the covers, in carousel themes.
ScreensaverYour games' screenshots or a blank screen, after 2 or 5 minutes.

Games

Aspect ratioOriginal, Integer pixels (sharp image) or Full screen.
Video filter, BezelsPicture effects and a frame around the game.
Duplicate gamesIf a game is in both emulators, show both or only one.
Games without ROMAlso shows catalog games you don't have.
Game genresRename and merge genres.
Performance profilePower saving extends battery life, Performance helps heavier games. It applies in games: when idle, the launcher slows down by itself to run cooler.
ControllersChooses whether an external controller is player 1 or 2.
Resume last gameIf you power off during a game, it starts again from there.

System

Wi-FiLeft and right turn it on and off; A shows the networks.
Date and time, Time zoneThe time also follows daylight saving.
Auto power offPowers off after a period without input, in the launcher.

Tools

Verify ROMsChecks that every zip contains the right files.
Download imagesCovers and information, over Wi-Fi.
Clean up imagesDeletes images of games no longer on the card.
Update systemDownloads the new version over Wi-Fi.
Diagnostic logHow much to write in Arcade/Logs, useful to track down a problem.
AboutSpace on the card and what uses it.

Per-game settings

From the in-game menu you can change aspect ratio, filter, bezel, rotation, options and controls for that game only.
