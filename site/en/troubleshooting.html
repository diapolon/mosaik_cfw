Black or red screen at startup

  1. Hold the power button for 10-20 seconds.
  2. Remove the card and look in the Arcade/Logs folder: avvio.log, sistema.txt and Arcade.log describe what happened.
  3. If the card is damaged, write the image again. Copy your saves to your computer first.

A game won't start

  • The message The core could not load the ROM means missing files or a ROM set other than the one required: see Loading games.
  • Run Verify ROMs and open the game's details page: it shows what's missing, including a BIOS.
  • Neo Geo games need neogeo.zip in Arcade/Bios.

A game is slow or the audio crackles

  • Try the Performance profile in Settings → Games.
  • Some 3D arcade games are too heavy for these consoles or aren't supported by the included emulators.
  • Try the same ROM with the other emulator, if it's in both.

The computer asks to format the card

Say no. The card contains boot partitions your computer can't read: that's normal. The one with your files is called ARCADE.

Damaged files or odd errors after copying

Cheap cards and readers can damage files without reporting errors. Try another reader and, if the problem persists, another microSD.

Covers won't download

Check that Wi-Fi is connected and that the date and time are right. Some lesser-known games may have no images.

Need more detail

Raise the level in Settings → Tools → Diagnostic log, reproduce the problem and look at the files in Arcade/Logs.
